RESEARCH WORKBENCH / TEN NEW CAPABILITIES

Ask more of your twin.

Compare hypotheses, inspect data, and follow each update with reproducible evidence.

01 / SCENARIO COMPARISON

Two possibilities. One history.

Pin a reference, change the controls, then simulate. Both scenarios are fitted on the current observations with the same horizon and interval.

No reference pinned yet.

02 / SCENARIO LIBRARY

Keep a useful hypothesis.

Save up to eight named settings in this browser. No observations or model results are saved. Use generic labels.

03 / DAILY TWIN UPDATE

A new day. An updated twin.

Append the next consecutive daily record. The updated history stays in this session and can be exported as CSV.

04 / DATA QUALITY REVIEW

Know what enters the model.

    Unusual readings, abrupt changes, constant features and highly correlated inputs are review prompts. No observations are automatically removed or corrected.

    05 / LONGITUDINAL TRENDS

    The direction behind the noise.

    06 / SENSITIVITY EXPLORER

    Change one thing at a time.

    Other scenario targets remain fixed. Sweep is limited to the intersection of observed values and allowed scenario bounds. Slopes are associations, not intervention effects.

    07 / ERROR DIAGNOSTICS

    Look where the model misses.

    DateObservedPredictedError

    Errors are actual minus predicted, in mmHg; positive bias means underprediction. All rows are from the chronological holdout.

    08 / UNCERTAINTY LENS

    A band with a clear meaning.

    Choose 80%, 90%, 95% or 99% in the scenario studio. The forecast band and holdout coverage update together; the predicted mean remains unchanged.

    Adjust the predictive interval ↑

    A wider interval expresses more uncertainty under the model assumptions. Changing it cannot improve the underlying model or establish calibration.

    09 / SHAREABLE SETTINGS

    Share the question.

    Create a link containing only scenario settings, horizon and interval level. It contains no observations, profile identity or results. Recipients apply it to their own active data.

    A local address works only on the machine running this app.

    10 / PRINTABLE RESEARCH REPORT

    Take the evidence with you.

    A clean report captures the active model, data provenance, scenario, forecast interval, holdout metrics, data-review findings and limitations.

    Use your browser’s PDF destination to save. Reports may contain personal information if you imported it; share only with authorization.

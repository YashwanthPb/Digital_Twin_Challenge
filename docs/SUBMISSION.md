## Ready-to-use project summary

PulseTwin is an open-source personal systolic blood-pressure digital twin for research. It combines interpretable Bayesian regression with a daily Kalman state update, then simulates user-defined lifestyle scenarios with predictive uncertainty. A local-first dashboard supports validated CSV imports, three reproducible synthetic profiles, chronological holdout comparisons and downloadable evidence. Synthetic holdout MAE is 2.81–3.19 mmHg across the included profiles. The prototype is not clinically validated and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice.
